Own Your PhD

A book focused on the psychological and professional development dimensions of doctoral study — the metaskills that influence how PhD students experience and sustain the process.

Courage. Resilience. Self-leadership. Self-regulation. These are not incidental to doctoral study — they shape whether it is survived, and whether it is worthwhile. Own Your PhD is written for doctoral students who want to understand and build these capacities deliberately, rather than leave them to chance.

More details, extracts and a publication timeline will be added here as the book develops.
