University Lecturer · Educator · Writer

Dimitar Karadzhov, PhD

I'm a University Lecturer and Deputy Programme Director in the School of Health & Wellbeing at the University of Glasgow, where I research and teach global mental health. Alongside that, I publish Deeply Human Skills, a newsletter on the metaskills that sustain meaningful work and personal growth, and I'm writing Own Your PhD, a book on the psychological dimensions of doctoral study.

The Scholar

Global Mental Health Research

My research focuses on personal recovery, homelessness and methodological and pedagogical innovation in global mental health, with work cited by the United Nations and in national policy reports.

Career Highlights:

  • PhD in Public Health and Health Policy
  • 10+ years in higher education
  • 25+ peer-reviewed discipline-spanning publications
  • Led ESRC-funded work
  • Academic achievement, teaching and knowledge exchange awards recipient
The Writer

Human-Centric Skills Development

Outside the university, I write about the durable, human-centred skills that shape how people do meaningful work and grow through difficulty — courage, resilience, self-leadership and self-regulation among them.

  • Deeply Human Skills — an infrequent (hope this changes!) newsletter on Substack
  • Own Your PhD — a book on the metaskills of doctoral study (coming soon)
