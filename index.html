University Lecturer · Educator · Writer

Dimitar Karadzhov, PhD

I'm a University Lecturer and Deputy Programme Director in the School of Health & Wellbeing at the University of Glasgow, where I research and teach global mental health. Alongside that, I write Deeply Human Skills, a newsletter on the metaskills that sustain meaningful work and personal growth, and I'm writing Own Your PhD, a book on the psychological dimensions of doctoral study.

The Scholar

Global mental health research

University Lecturer, School of Health & Wellbeing, University of Glasgow, and Deputy Programme Director of the MSc Global Mental Health. My research focuses on personal recovery, homelessness, and methodological innovation in global mental health, with work cited by the United Nations and in national policy reports.

  • PhD in Public Health and Health Policy, University of Strathclyde
  • 19+ peer-reviewed publications
  • Teaching and Knowledge Exchange award recipient
The Writer

Deeply Human Skills

Outside the university, I write about the durable, human-centred skills that shape how people do meaningful work and grow through difficulty — courage, resilience, self-leadership and self-regulation among them.

  • Deeply Human Skills — an infrequent (hope this changes!) newsletter on Substack
  • Own Your PhD — a book on the metaskills of doctoral study (coming soon)
